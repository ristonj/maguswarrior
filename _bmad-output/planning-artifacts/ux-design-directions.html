Card hierarchy: Hand cards (dim border, neutral) → Staged cards (gold border, warm glow, effect label) → the visual jump signals a state change, not just a position change. The staged zone is fixed-width so playing 4 attack cards never pushes Source/Mana/Crystals off screen. Staged cards scroll within their zone if needed.
Hand scroll: 7 cards comfortable. 8th card partially clips + fade gradient + › signals more. Player swipes hand row to reach them. Wound cards count toward the limit and scroll like any other.
HUD totals (Move: 2) are the output of staged cards — shown at top because you need them while looking at the map. No redundant total in the bottom strip.
Strengths
Maximum map height — most of screen is exploration space
Staged zone is fixed — adding cards never displaces mana economy
Three mana zones (Source · Mana · Crystals) correctly model the economy
Hand scroll is expected mobile behaviour — 7 cards is generous
Unit zone scales to level 7+ with horizontal scroll
HUD is clean: running totals only, no redundant display
Tradeoffs
Bottom strip is 128dp — map canvas loses 18dp vs. original 110dp
Scrollable hand means some cards are hidden by default
Card offers require deliberate ☰ tap — no ambient offer visibility
